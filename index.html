About

Mapping the world,
one dataset at a time.

I'm a GIS & Remote Sensing specialist from Chiatura, Georgia with deep expertise in geospatial analysis, spatial data infrastructure, and open-source GIS tools.

I develop QGIS plugins, Python geospatial packages, and interactive web maps — turning complex spatial data into actionable insights.

My work spans urban 3D city modelling (CityGML), satellite image analysis, cloud GIS infrastructure, and educational GIS platforms.

Active contributor to the open-source geospatial community and instructor at BTU (Business and Technology University).
